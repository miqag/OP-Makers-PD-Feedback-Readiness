Method & notes

Source

A single post-session reflection form completed by 24 leaders. The form contained 11 Baseline Assessment indicators grouped under Belonging (3), Consistency (5), and Coherence (3), plus three open-ended questions.

Scoring

Averages convert the four response options to points: Never = 1, Sometimes = 2, Most of the time = 3, Always = 4. Averages are used only to rank and compare indicators; the stacked bars remain the primary read, since an average can hide a split group.

Completeness

One rating was left blank (Consistency — "In team meetings, teachers commit to concrete actions for individual students"), so that indicator reflects 23 responses rather than 24. Two leaders left the "doing well" question blank and three left the "support needed" question blank. Blanks are excluded from averages, never counted as zero.

Levels

Levels are as leaders self-reported them. Subgroups are small — between 2 and 7 people — so level comparisons are directional only and should not be read as significant differences. One leader left the level blank and one identified by a specific building role; both are grouped as "Not specified."

Anonymity

Respondent names have been removed. Within quotes, references to the district, individual buildings, and named colleagues are replaced with bracketed placeholders. Quotes are otherwise reproduced exactly as written, including original spelling.

Theme coding

Themes were derived from the responses themselves rather than applied from a preset list. Because responses often address more than one idea, theme counts sum to more than the number of respondents.

Based solely on the 24 submitted reflection forms. Ratings, counts, and quotes are reproduced from the source responses; no data has been supplemented or estimated.
